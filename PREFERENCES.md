## Design taste
- Clean, calm and restrained: white or light themes, MUJI-like simplicity, granite and white, editorial colours.
- No loud designs, dark neon palettes, dense nested boxes, or visual clutter. Use clear dividers between sections and metrics.
- Keep my name quiet and small. Let the work lead.
- Subtle interactivity is welcome, like a faint, calm animated background.
- Keep sections short: one icon and one line per item beats long write-ups. Show only the strongest items.
- Put a minus sign on negative numbers, and make status colours obvious (green for up, red for down, grey for flat).
- Work well on phone and desktop.
- Tools: frontend-design, impeccable (run `impeccable detect` and aim for zero findings) and shadcn/ui. Pull real references first (see `skills/web-design-inspiration`, plus Mobbin).
